# Claim 3 method

At all zero, the lower player’s strict-overtake effort has infimum `1/2`,
cost zero, which is strictly below the reward gap `1/10`. At `[1,1]`, the
higher player already gets maximum reward at zero cost. The lower player needs
effort strictly above `3/2` to win, which costs strictly above `1/4`, more than
the reward. Z3 quantifies over every unilateral nonnegative deviation.
