# Claim 4 source audit

Proposition 5.3 itself is conditional monotonicity: if all-zero is a PNE at a
smaller TbT level, it remains one at a larger level. The stronger unique-PNE
claim appears in the abstract and is obtained only by combining Section 5 with
Theorem 4.6. The counterexample targets that aggregate unique-equilibrium claim
and explicitly does not call the conditional proposition false.
