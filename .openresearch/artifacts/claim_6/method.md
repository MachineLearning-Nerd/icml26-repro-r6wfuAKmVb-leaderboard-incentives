# Claim 6 method

The fixed verifier downloads the exact hashed arXiv source with an explicit
User-Agent and performs four routes:

1. Calibrate the unique 30-segment red vector path against the PDF’s own
   labeled x/y tick geometry and decode its endpoint.
2. Hold out the 3,000-step endpoint and extrapolate it from the preceding 30
   vector points, reporting degree sensitivity rather than only one fit.
3. Independently confirm the exact number and quantifiers in three TeX anchors.
4. Seek a source-level falsification by scanning for an alternate curve or
   coordinate contradiction.

These routes test publication consistency. They do not recreate the missing
training measurements, so they cannot verify the empirical fit itself.
